Severity levels. Severity 1 is a full outage or data loss, severity 2 is a major feature unavailable with no workaround, severity 3 is a degraded feature with a workaround, and severity 4 is a question or a cosmetic issue. The severity you select when opening a ticket sets the response target.

Response targets by plan. Enterprise severity 1 is answered within one hour and severity 2 within four hours, around the clock. Pro tickets of any severity are answered within four business hours. Free tickets have no response target. Targets count from the moment the ticket is created, not from the first reply.
