Pro plan support. Pro includes email and in-app chat support 24 hours a day, Monday to Friday, with a first-response target of four business hours. Pro customers can open up to 20 tickets per month, escalate one ticket per month to a senior engineer, and join the monthly office-hours call. Phone support and a named account manager are not included in Pro; they are Enterprise features.

Enterprise plan support. Enterprise includes 24x7 phone, chat and email support, a one-hour first-response target for severity-1 incidents, a named technical account manager, unlimited tickets and a quarterly architecture review.

Free plan support. Free includes the community forum and the documentation site. Email tickets are answered on a best-effort basis with no response-time target.
