Refunds. Pro subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. Contact billing to request one; refunds are issued to the original payment method within five business days.

Invoices. Invoices are issued on the first of each month and are available under Settings, Billing. Enterprise customers may request consolidated quarterly invoices.
