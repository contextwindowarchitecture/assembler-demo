Pro plan support. Pro includes email and in-app chat support 24 hours a day, Monday to Friday, with a first-response target of four business hours. Pro customers can open up to 20 tickets per month, escalate one ticket per month to a senior engineer, and join the monthly office-hours call. Phone support and a named account manager are not included in Pro; they are Enterprise features. Support is closed on the public holidays listed in the customer portal.

Enterprise support hours. Enterprise support operates 24x7, including public holidays.
