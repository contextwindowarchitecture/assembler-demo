Escalating a ticket. Pro customers may escalate one open ticket per calendar month to a senior support engineer from the ticket page. Enterprise customers may escalate any ticket at any time through their technical account manager. Escalation does not change the severity or the response target; it changes who works the ticket.

What escalation is not. An escalation is not a way to request a feature or to reopen a closed ticket. Reopen a closed ticket from the ticket page within 14 days of closure.
