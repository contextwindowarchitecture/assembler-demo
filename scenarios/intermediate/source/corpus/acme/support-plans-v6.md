Pro plan support, 2025 edition. Pro includes business-hours phone support and email support with a two-business-hour first-response target, and up to 10 tickets per month. Escalations are handled by the on-call engineer.

Enterprise plan support, 2025 edition. Enterprise includes 24x7 phone support, a dedicated account manager and unlimited tickets.
