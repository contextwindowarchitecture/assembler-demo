CWA support-assistant demo · one question, three assemblers, every decision on one screen

Each stage is its own page with its own frozen scenarios. They share the four columns: candidate context → CWA decisions → outbound request → model answer.

Basic

“What support does the Pro plan include?”

Show me exactly what CWA assembled. Local fixtures, five cumulative steps: a clean assembly, stale and foreign documents, authority boundaries, budget pressure, and a refusal.

Proves admission, authority boundaries, budgeting, rendering and reproducibility across Python, TypeScript and Go.

Intermediate

“Given my account and our previous conversation, what support am I entitled to?”

Several sources compete for a limited budget. LlamaIndex retrieval and postprocessing over a corpus of support documents, an account database, a memory store and the conversation, run live or replayed from the captured batches.

Proves declared conflicts, deduplication, source diversity, supplied summaries, and the route's required-evidence behaviour.

Advanced

“Check my entitlement, investigate this incident, and draft the next action.”

Context evolves through a bounded tool loop. Three MCP servers propose tools, the capability policy grants some, the guard authorizes every request before a call, and each observation enters the next inference through the context boundary. Recorded runs replay through all three assemblers.

Proves that every inference has a replayable snapshot, tool results are evidence, unauthorized calls never execute, and recovery terminates.
Arrow keys switch steps on a stage page. Providers come from .env.
